About PixelBlue

We started PixelBlue in March 2026 to make our own software: small, focused apps for the everyday things people do over and over.

Our story

PixelBlue LLC is a California limited liability company. We design, build and publish our own apps, from the first sketch to the App Store and the Mac. Our founder trained in electronic information engineering and computer engineering, with a background in software architecture, database systems, machine learning and mobile development.

Each product starts with a small problem we run into ourselves. Prec is for the bag you pack again and again. TandemDock came from working across several displays every day, with the Dock on only one of them. Larger software companies tend to skip problems like these because each one looks small. To the people who live with them, they aren’t.

We release one product at a time, listen to the people who use it, and keep improving what they come back to.

How we work

Start small

Every feature has to earn its place. When in doubt, we leave it out.

Respect attention

No streaks, no guilt notifications, no manufactured urgency. Our apps show up when they’re useful and get out of the way.

Keep data close

We collect as little as we can. Prec keeps your lists on your device, and TandemDock works without an account. Neither has analytics.

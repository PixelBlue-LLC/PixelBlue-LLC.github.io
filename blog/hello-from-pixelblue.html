Hello from PixelBlue

Who we are, what we’re building, and why we chose small, specific problems.

PixelBlue LLC was founded in March 2026 in Sacramento, California. We’re a small software studio, and this is where we’ll share what we’re working on.

What we build

We focus on everyday coordination problems that general-purpose apps tend to overlook: getting ready for things you do over and over, handing off care between family members, and planning a day of cleaning appointments across town.

Our first product is Prec, a reusable preparation checklist for iPhone and iPad, available on the App Store. Next, we’re researching a coordination app for families who share caregiving, and later a scheduling and routing tool for independent housekeepers.

How we work

We keep things lean. Client projects fund product development, we launch one product at a time, and we only invest more when people actually keep using what we’ve built.

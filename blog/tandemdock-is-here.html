TandemDock is here

With more than one display, your Mac shows the Dock on only one of them at a time. TandemDock, our second product, puts a Dock on every display. It’s free to download today.

Why we built it

We work across several screens every day, and the Dock is where almost everything starts: opening an app, switching to it, dropping a file on it. macOS shows the Dock on one display at a time. To use it on another screen, you move the pointer to the bottom of that screen and wait for the Dock to move over, and then it’s gone from the screen you came from.

There are apps that replace the Dock with their own. We wanted something gentler: keep the real macOS Dock exactly as it is, and give each of the other displays a Dock that works in tandem with it.

What it does

  • A Dock on every other display. The same apps in the same order, with running indicators, badges and today’s date on Calendar.
  • Follows your settings. Size, magnification, auto-hide and bounce all match what you chose in System Settings › Desktop & Dock, and change when you change them.
  • Feels native. It uses Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and keeps windows from sliding under it.
  • Your layout. Put the Dock at the bottom, left or right of each display, independently of the main one.

Free, with an optional Pro

Everything above is free, on as many displays as you have. TandemDock Pro adds window previews when you hover an app, on its own Docks and on the macOS Dock, and can give each display only the apps that have windows there.

Every new install includes a 14-day Pro trial, with no card needed. Pro purchases open soon: it will be a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

No account, no tracking

TandemDock doesn’t collect any data. It goes online only to check for updates, and to check a Pro license if you have one. The details are in the privacy policy.

Get it

TandemDock runs on macOS 14 or later. Download it here, drag it to Applications and open it; a short guide sets it up, and it lives in your menu bar from then on. Updates arrive automatically. More on the TandemDock page.

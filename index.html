What we’re building

Each product solves one specific coordination problem that general-purpose tools leave behind.

Prec

A calm, reusable preparation checklist for iPhone and iPad. Build a list once for work, the gym, the pool or a weekend away, check it off before you leave, and tap “Ready for Next Time” to reset it. Prec learns what you usually bring, drafts new lists on your device, and keeps your data private.

TandemDock

A Dock for every display of your Mac. With more than one display, macOS shows the Dock on just one of them; TandemDock adds one to each of the others that looks and works like the real Dock and follows your Dock settings.

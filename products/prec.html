Prec

Your stuff, ready every time.

Prec is a reusable preparation checklist for iPhone and iPad. Make a list once for the things you do over and over, check it before you leave, and reset it for next time.

How it works

  1. Set it up once

    Make a list for Work, the gym, the pool or a weekend away. Start from a template or a few words.

  2. Check it before you leave

    Tick things off as you pack. A reminder and a Home Screen widget can nudge you at the right moment.

  3. Ready for next time

    One tap clears the checks. Tomorrow the list is waiting, exactly as you set it up.

Quietly learns how you pack

Prec Remembers

Tell it what you can never leave without, and it checks every list for it. It notices what you always bring and what you never use, and asks before changing anything.

Asking

Never done it before? Describe what you’re getting ready for and Prec drafts a list for you to edit and keep.

Both run on your device. Asking uses Apple Intelligence where it’s available; other devices get a draft from built-in lists.

Your lists stay yours

No account, no analytics, no ads. PixelBlue doesn’t collect any data from Prec.

Lists live on your device, and in your own private iCloud only if you turn on iCloud Sync.

Release notes

  1. 2.0Oct 2026

    Prec learns how you pack, quietly and on your device.

    • Prec Remembers. Tell Prec what you can never leave without, such as medicine, glasses or keys. It checks every list for it and names it in reminders. It also notices what you always pack and what you never use, and asks before changing anything.
    • Asking. Describe what you’re getting ready for and Prec drafts a list for you to review. Uses Apple Intelligence on supported devices; everything stays on your device.
    • iCloud Sync. If you choose, your lists on all your devices, through your own iCloud.
    • iPad. Lists on the left, the open list on the right.
    • Larger Text. Prec now follows your text size setting.
  2. 1.2Oct 2026

    Everything we’d been working on since launch.

    • Reminders. Daily, weekly, monthly, custom or one-time, with an optional heads-up. Lists can reset themselves when the reminder comes around.
    • Home Screen widget. Your next list and what’s still to pack, on a chalkboard (clean, cat or dog).
    • See when each list was last reset.
    • Sections and sub-steps for longer lists.
    • Quick-Add library of common items, plus Check All.
    • Swipe to delete, long-press to pin, rename lists and items, pick any emoji.
    • Dark Mode and a new Settings screen.
    • Many fixes and polish.
  3. 1.0May 2026

    First release.

    • Reusable checklists for the things you do over and over, with a one-tap “Ready for Next Time” reset. Your lists stay on your device.

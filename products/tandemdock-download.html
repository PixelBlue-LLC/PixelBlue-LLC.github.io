Set it up

  1. Install

    Open TandemDock.dmg from your Downloads folder and drag TandemDock to Applications.

  2. Open it from Applications

    A short welcome guide walks you through setup. TandemDock lives in your menu bar, not in the Dock.

  3. Allow Accessibility access

    When asked, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and turn on TandemDock. It uses this only to keep windows clear of its Docks, on your Mac. If TandemDock is already in the list but doesn’t seem to work, turn it off and on again.

Window previews in Pro also ask for Screen Recording. That’s optional; previews work without thumbnails if you skip it.

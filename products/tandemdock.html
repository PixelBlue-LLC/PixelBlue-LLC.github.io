TandemDock

Your Dock, on every display.

With more than one display, macOS shows the Dock on just one of them. TandemDock adds a Dock to each of the others that works in tandem with the real one: same apps, same settings, always one click away.

How it works

  1. Open it once

    A short welcome guide sets it up and asks for the one permission it needs. TandemDock lives in your menu bar.

  2. Use your Dock as usual

    Pin apps, change the size, turn on magnification. TandemDock follows your Dock settings within seconds.

  3. Every display gets one

    Plug in a display and its Dock appears. Unplug it and it’s gone. Your main Dock stays exactly as it was.

Feels like the real Dock

Your apps, your settings

The same apps in the same order, with running indicators, badges and today’s date on Calendar. Size, magnification, auto-hide and bounce all match what you chose in Desktop & Dock.

Windows stay clear

Windows that slide under the Dock are moved out of the way, so nothing hides behind it.

Liquid Glass

On macOS 26, TandemDock uses the same Liquid Glass material as the Dock it sits beside.

Put the Dock at the bottom, left or right of your other displays, independently of the main one.

TandemDock Pro

A one-time purchase, no subscription. Hover an app to see its windows and jump to any of them, and give each display only the apps that have windows there. More is on the way.

Stays on your Mac

No account, no analytics, no ads. PixelBlue doesn’t collect any data from TandemDock.

TandemDock uses Accessibility access only to arrange your Dock and windows, on your Mac. Window previews in Pro can show thumbnails if you allow Screen Recording; they are never saved or sent anywhere. The only time TandemDock goes online is to check a Pro license.

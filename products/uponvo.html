How it works

  1. Allow access once

    Uponvo reads the calendars and reminders on your iPhone, including iCloud, Google, Exchange and any account you’ve added in Settings. Nothing to sign in to, nothing to sync.

  2. Add a widget

    Small or medium on the Home Screen; rectangular, circular or a single line on the Lock Screen.

  3. Glance and go

    See what’s next and how long until it starts. Tap to open the details. Tapping never completes a reminder by accident.

Free to use, Pro if you want more

The small Home Screen widget, all three Lock Screen widgets and the list in the app are free.

Uponvo Pro adds:

  • The medium Home Screen widget
  • Widgets for events only or reminders only, and overdue reminders that stay on the widget
  • Faster widget refresh: every 15, 20 or 30 minutes, during the hours you choose
  • Refresh right after you edit, with a Shortcuts automation
  • Pick which calendars and lists to show; group the list by day or week
  • Full event details, and jump straight to a reminder in Reminders

$1.99 a month, $12.99 a year, or $20.99 once. Prices are in US dollars and may differ in your country.

Your data stays on your iPhone

No account, no analytics, no ads. PixelBlue doesn’t collect any data from Uponvo.

Your events and reminders are read on your device and never sent anywhere.
